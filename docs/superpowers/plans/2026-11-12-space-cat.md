# Space Cat Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build Space Cat — a four-lane flying sums game adapted (by permission) from Igor's *Space Cat Math* — as a
fourth Vite entry in alef, with an adaptive sums ladder at 1 mistake in 5, unseen speed adaptation, a parent area,
its voice lines, and a card on the home screen that *one duel* builds.

**Architecture:** A separate Vite entry (`spacecat/index.html` → `src/spacecat/`) in the layout of the improvement
work (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-16-improvement.md`, branch `feature/improve`): the root goes through the shared
game shell (`src/shared/shell/`), the parent screens are Space Cat's part of the one parent area (`src/parent/`,
declared in `src/spacecat/parent/index.ts`), behind the shared parent gate. Its own persisted save and telemetry log.
Pure, unit-tested logic in `src/spacecat/logic/` (items and ladder, prediction on top of `src/shared/adaptive.ts`, wrong
answers, speed, the round state machine as a pure `step(state, event)`, the run) feeds an imperative canvas engine
(`src/spacecat/canvas/`, ported from the original). A framework-free `PlayController` (`src/spacecat/controller.ts`,
seams: the engine, the audio, a save port, the log, a `Sleeper`) runs the flow; `usePlay.ts` is its thin React
bridge, as `src/duel2/useRun.ts` is for `RunController`. Voice lines play through the shared game-audio kit
(`createGameAudio`, `src/shared/gameAudio/`); the coded effects and the tune are WebAudio on the player port's output
node, so the device's sound setting, the mute on hide and the pause hold reach them with no Howler import.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5.9, React 19, Vite 7, Zustand 5 (persist), Howler 2.2 (only through the kit's adapter),
Canvas 2D, WebAudio, Vitest 4 + Testing Library (jsdom), ESLint 9, dependency-cruiser, knip. Host pipeline for voice: the existing host-local ElevenLabs pipeline of Book 2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-space-cat-design.md` (read it before any task). Rules:
`docs/minigame-rules.md` (read it before any UI, audio or asset task). Map and words: `CLAUDE.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`,
`docs/adr/`, `src/shared/README.md` and the READMEs of `src/duel2/` and `src/clock/` (their *Known friction* says what
not to copy). Every task's requirements include all of them.

## Review Focus

1. **The tablet locks or the app changes in the middle of a zoom, a demo, a tip or a voice line:** expect all sound to
   stop, the opaque pause cover, and on return the same sum from the top with no heart lost, the round recorded as cut
   and not tallied, and the demo or tip shown again from its start. Tests: Task 15 `a pause during a zoom restarts the
   same sum and costs no heart`, `a pause during the demo replays the demo`.
2. **Canvas text the DOM scan cannot see:** a stray label, a "?" or a NaN drawn on the canvas. Expect only digits and the
   signs. Tests: Task 13 `drawText records every string`, Task 22 scan of the recorded strings.
3. **Touches at the edges of a round:** a flick and an arrival in the same frame, a tap on a planet during the demo
   line, a drag that ends exactly between two lanes at arrival, a second zoom during a zoom, keys held down. Expect one
   result per round, never a heart for parking between planets. Tests: Task 8 `arrival during a zoom waits for the hit`,
   `between lanes resolves to the nearest lane, ties to the ship's lane`; Task 12 `held key repeats do not skip lanes`.
4. **A save that is missing, old or unreadable, and settings that conflict** (the parent caps the range below the
   open rungs, turns times off after rung 7 opened, fixes the time then goes back to automatic). Expect a fresh save
   from bad JSON, candidates only from rungs that are open and allowed, and the automatic time continuing from its last
   value. Tests: Task 10A `bad JSON gives a fresh save`, Task 5 `turning times off closes rungs 7 to 9 for the choice`.
5. **No sound at all** (voice files not generated yet, AudioContext still suspended, a slow phone): expect the game to
   flow silently and in time; a missing line never blocks the planets. Tests: Task 14B `say resolves at once for an
   unknown id`, Task 15 `a whole level plays with no audio files`.
