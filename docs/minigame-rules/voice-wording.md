# Voice lines: wording

- Use natural, spoken Hebrew: the phrase that people really say, even when it breaks a pattern. Where two phrases are
  common, use both. When the parent gives a wording, use it.
  Example (clock): nobody says "עֶשְׂרִים וְחָמֵשׁ לְשֶׁבַע" or "twenty-five to eight"; people say "שֵׁשׁ שְׁלוֹשִׁים וְחָמֵשׁ" and
  "seven thirty-five". For :40, both "twenty to two" and "one forty" are common, so the game uses both.
  Examples (tower): "מגה כישוף" (not "כישוף מגה"), "נשחק שוב?", "וואו, אתה בשוונג".
- Write each line as what an adult says to the child at that moment, in the order that the child hears it. Before you
  generate, write out each sequence (what plays before, the line, what plays after) and read it aloud as a whole. Then
  have a native reviewer (a person, or a model told to act as an Israeli kindergarten teacher) check each line in its
  sequence and its nikkud.
  Reason: the parent found the instructions "unnatural and out of place" in every game.
  Example (Hebrew Spell Quest): "תִּתְפֹּס אֶת מָה שֶׁתִּשְׁמַע." + [בָּ] became "אֵיפֹה כָּתוּב..." + [בָּ]; "the word on the gate" for a door became
  "מָה כָּתוּב עַל הַדֶּלֶת?"; the dotted-letter introduction says "בְּלִי נְקֻדָּה..." + [וָ], "וְעִם נְקֻדָּה..." + [בָּ].
- Let a line that a sound follows lead into it. The speaker says "what I say", not "what you will hear". Do not end it
  with "…": the voice made a moan at the end. End it with ":" or "." and listen to the end of each take.
  Example (Hebrew Spell Quest): "בְּלִי נְקֻדָּה..." was "too moany". The plain spelling "ועם נקודה" was read "ve'en" (ואין, the opposite):
  keep nikkud on a word that the plain spelling makes ambiguous.
- Make sure each line is correct in its context, and that its feeling agrees with the moment.
  Example (tower): "המגדל נפל. כל הכבוד" praised the child for a loss. The first "כִּמְעַט" sounded disappointed; it must encourage.
  Example (Hebrew Spell Quest): "הַמַּטְבְּעוֹת שֶׁלְּךָ שָׁוִים כָּפוּל" was wrong: the coins are not worth more, he gets more coins. The line is
  now "עַכְשָׁו כָּל תְּשׁוּבָה נְכוֹנָה נוֹתֶנֶת פִּי שְׁנַיִם מַטְבְּעוֹת".
- A line must not point at a thing that is not on the screen ("this", "here", "that one"). If the thing is not shown
  yet, name it. Example (clock): a customer said "אַתָּה יוֹדֵעַ אֵיךְ מְכַוְּנִים אֶת זֶה?" before its clock was on the bench;
  now it says "הַשָּׁעוֹן שֶׁלִּי מַרְאֶה שָׁעָה לֹא נְכוֹנָה".
- Make an announcement say something specific. Make the next round show it.
  Example (tower): "תראה, מפלצת חדשה" came before a monster that he saw before. "מעכשיו צריך גם להקשיב" was not clear, and the
  next round was a picture round.
- Give a line that plays again and again several variations, and do not play the same variation twice in a row.
  Example (clock): every customer said "הַשָּׁעוֹן שֶׁלִּי הִתְקַלְקֵל"; now each has five hellos.
- Speak to each child in his or her gender. Give every line that addresses the child (verbs, pronouns, adjectives, the
  player's own character) a feminine version, and show a girl a girl character everywhere. Keep one take for a line that
  does not address the child. Example (all games): a girl hears תִּתְפְּסִי and sees the girl wizard on the broom, on the menu card and on the end screen.
- Make each number agree in gender with the noun that it counts. Check every line with a number and a noun.
  Example (a merchant draft): מַטְבֵּעַ is masculine, so "עֲשָׂרָה מַטְבְּעוֹת", not "עֶשֶׂר מַטְבְּעוֹת".
- Do not put a number in a line if the game logic can change that number.
  Example (tower): the boss line said "3 spells". Then the boss got 8 hit points.
- Do not use game or technical words. Say what happens.
  Example (tower): "wave" and "combo" were removed. "עוד מפלצות בדרך" replaced the wave line.
- Use the full name of a thing when its short name has another meaning. Example (Hebrew Spell Quest): רוּחַ alone is "wind"; the
  ghosts are רוּחוֹת רְפָאִים.
- In a bilingual game, keep the child in one language for a block of questions (the non-English language first), then
  change. Do not switch the language at every question. Example (clock): a workday is 4 Hebrew customers, then 4 English.
- In captions, use the spelling that the child sees in books (full spelling, with vav where the reading has it).
  Example (clock): "הַמָּחוֹג הָאָרוֹךְ", not "הָאָרֹךְ".
- Record a word alone. Do not put it in a carrier phrase.
  Example (tower): "כְּמוֹ אַרְיֵה" was rejected. "אַרְיֵה." was kept.
